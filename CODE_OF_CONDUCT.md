# Code of Conduct

RustDroid welcomes people who use, question, and improve the project in good faith. We commit to a respectful, harassment-free space across issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project channels.

## Expected behaviour

- Focus criticism on code, documentation, evidence, and ideas.
- Be patient with different experience levels and communicate assumptions clearly.
- Respect privacy: redact APK contents, keys, personal data, and private logs before posting.
- Accept a maintainer decision when it is explained in terms of scope, safety, or evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, deliberate disruption, or publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through GitHub's private contact channel. A report should include the relevant link, time, and a short factual description. Maintainers may remove content, warn, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban an account. Security vulnerabilities belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public conduct report.
